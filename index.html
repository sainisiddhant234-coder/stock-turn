What is Stock Turn?

Stock Turn (also called Inventory Turnover or Stockturn) tells you how many times your business sells and replaces its entire inventory during a given period.

Stock Turn = COGS ÷ Average Inventory

  • Higher number → inventory is moving fast (usually good for cash flow)
  • Lower number → stock is sitting longer (risk of overstock, obsolescence, or slow sales)

Typical targets vary by industry: grocery & fast fashion often aim for 8–12+, fashion retail 4–6, many other retailers 3–8.
